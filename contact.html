Common questions

Before You Reach Out

What does the strategy call cover?

A free 30-minute session where we discuss your challenge, assess whether sovereign AI deployment is feasible, and outline possible approaches. No commitment required.

What's a typical engagement timeline?

Most projects run from 90 days to 24 months depending on scope. Digitization projects, sovereign model deployments and advisory engagements each follow structured delivery phases.

Do you work with governments?

Yes. Government ministries and national institutions are our primary B2G focus. We specialise in sovereign infrastructure for public sector institutions across West Africa.

Is our data kept within our country?

Always. Sovereign data residency is not optional in our engagements: every system we deploy runs on infrastructure within your national jurisdiction, under your governance and your law.
