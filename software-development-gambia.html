Common questions

Software development FAQ

Is HexAI a software development company in The Gambia?

Yes. HexAI is based in Banjul and builds custom software for Gambian businesses, associations and government, including the payment gateway, business systems and public platforms shown on this page.

Can you add mobile money payments to our app or website?

Yes. We run our own payment gateway, HPG, which handles collections and payouts over Wave, APS and cards through one API. We can integrate it into new or existing software.

Do you maintain software after launch?

Yes. The systems on this page are maintained for the clients using them daily. We agree support and maintenance terms as part of every project.

How long does a software project take, and what does it cost?

It depends on scope. After a free 30-minute strategy call we scope the work and give you a timeline and quote. Smaller systems can launch in weeks; larger platforms run in phases.
