Common questions

AI development FAQ

Does HexAI build AI in The Gambia, or outsource it?

We build it ourselves. HexAI's engineering team is based in Banjul and has fine-tuned, deployed and maintains the AI products shown on this page.

Can AI work for users with poor or no internet?

Yes. Bantaba AI runs a fine-tuned model entirely on a budget Android phone, and Base10 works offline and syncs when students reconnect. We design for the connectivity your users actually have.

Do we need a lot of data to start?

Not always. Many projects start from an existing open model fine-tuned on a modest amount of your data. During scoping we tell you honestly what data is needed and whether it is available.

How much does an AI project cost?

It depends on scope, so we quote per project after a free 30-minute strategy call. We'll recommend the simplest approach that solves the problem, which is often cheaper than you expect.
