01 · EdTech & AI tutoringLive · Free beta

Karang Maths, but make it click.

Karang is an AI maths tutor that adapts to every learner: kids, teens and adults. Its tutor, Bantaba, builds each lesson around the learner's age, grade, country, pace and interests, using examples from their own world. It started as a tutor for WASSCE prep and Grade 4 maths, and is designed to feel local first while working anywhere.

  • Three modes: Kids (6 to 11) with big tap-to-answer tiles, Teen (12 to 15) with step-by-step lessons, and a quiet, focused Adult mode for exam prep
  • Socratic hints that guide learners to the answer instead of giving it away
  • Worked examples in LaTeX, adaptive quiz feedback and a sidebar chat for follow-up questions
  • Tracks strengths and weaknesses by concept and adapts to slow, normal or fast pace
  • Local context: familiar currencies and everyday items for The Gambia, Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa

02 · Fintech & paymentsLive in The Gambia

HexAI Payment Gateway Every dalasi, on one live ledger.

One API for collections and payouts across Wave, APS, and cards, with a live ledger as the single source of truth for the money moving through a business. Plain REST and JSON with no SDK required, documented in an OpenAPI spec that is checked in CI so the docs can't drift from the API. Pricing is a single commission per successful transaction: no setup fee, no monthly fee, nothing charged on failed payments.

  • Collections: Wave, APS and card checkout from a single request, hosted and reconciled
  • Payouts that settle on each merchant's configured schedule
  • Every event delivered by webhook, signed with HMAC-SHA256, with automatic redelivery
  • Three requests is the whole integration; quickstart to a first payment in about 10 minutes
  • Sandbox and test keys, dashboard, analytics, team access and KYC onboarding included

03 · Business software & accountingLive

Kernel The business operating system for African SMEs.

Kernel was born from a real failure. In Q1 2026 a client asked HexAI for every invoice ever issued to them, and we couldn't produce it: the records lived on a single laptop that became corrupted. Kernel makes that impossible. It covers invoices, bills, contacts, payments, bank reconciliation and audit-grade reporting, so a pharmacy in Serrekunda with a part-time bookkeeper never loses its financial history. HexAI is customer zero.

  • End-to-end finance: invoices, bills, contacts, payments and bank transactions
  • Immutable audit logs and reporting, with no data trapped on one device or in one person's head
  • Encrypted document vault for contracts, receipts and financial records
  • Built for low-bandwidth West Africa with server rendering and resilient sync
  • Next.js App Router, PostgreSQL, Prisma and JWT, deployed on DigitalOcean

04 · GovTech & conservationLiveClient · DPWM

Gambia's Biodiversity Outlook Built for the Department of Parks and Wildlife Management.

The national platform for The Gambia's protected-area network. Built for DPWM, it brings national parks, reserves, community-conserved areas (ICCAs), OECM sites and the Niumi UNESCO Biosphere Reserve into one public map and data portal, so citizens, researchers and partners can see what is protected, where, and by whom.

  • Interactive map of every protected area, filterable by national parks, ICCAs, OECMs and biosphere
  • Explore by region: each administrative region lists its parks, reserves and community-conserved areas
  • Live coverage dashboard: national, terrestrial, coastal and marine, and OECM coverage
  • OECM and biosphere profiles with hectares, location and governance context
  • Partner directory linking DPWM, UNESCO, the University of The Gambia and NGOs

05 · AI & offline learningLive · APK v1.1.1

Bantaba AI A Socratic tutor. Think. Discover. Learn.

Bantaba AI is a Socratic tutor for data science and machine learning that never just hands over the answer. It guides learners with questions and small hints, and runs fully offline on a budget Android phone once the model is downloaded. Built for students with unreliable connectivity, it switches between on-device and online modes automatically.

  • Socratic chat that detects whether a question is conceptual, about code, or casual, and responds with guiding questions
  • On-device inference: a fine-tuned Qwen3-0.6B, quantised to GGUF Q4_K_M, running through llama.cpp compiled for ARM64
  • Hybrid routing between offline and online modes, with guards against crashes and out-of-memory errors
  • Bundled DS/ML curriculum, adaptive quizzes and an in-app Python playground powered by Pyodide
  • Flutter app with dart:ffi bindings to a custom C API, plus a FastAPI backend for online mode

09 · Civic tech & fundraisingLive

Kambeng The Gambia's transparent crowdfunding platform.

Kambeng ("community" in Mandinka) lets Gambians fund what matters: real causes, real proof. Anyone can launch a campaign and collect donations by Wave, APS Mobile Money or card, then prove every dalasi was well spent with photos and receipts their donors can see. Donating takes under two minutes and needs no account.

  • Four steps from idea to impact: create a campaign, get ID-verified, share and collect, then prove and withdraw
  • Every campaigner passes a one-time national ID (KYC) check, usually within 24 hours
  • Donations by Wave, APS Mobile Money or Visa/Mastercard through the HexAI Payment Gateway
  • Withdrawals tied to uploaded receipts and photo proof that donors can see
  • Shareable campaign links and QR codes, plus a recurring-donation scheduler

10 · Fintech & SME analytics

FinsaFi OCR-powered financial analytics for microenterprises.

FinsaFi turns a shoebox of paper receipts into clean financial data. Owners snap or upload a receipt and PaddleOCR extracts the transaction, with duplicate detection and validation to keep the books trustworthy. Gemini adds plain-language insights, and P&L, balance sheet and cash flow reports export to PDF and Excel.

  • Receipt OCR with PaddleOCR (PP-OCRv4) for JPG, PNG and PDF
  • Hash-based duplicate detection and anomaly validation
  • AI insights and recommendations powered by Google Gemini
  • Offline-safe upload queueing and organisation-level access control
