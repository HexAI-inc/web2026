REST · JSON · OpenAPI

From sign-up to first payment in an afternoon

Plain REST and JSON, no SDK required. Amounts are decimal strings, requests are idempotent by reference, and the API is documented in an OpenAPI spec that is checked in CI, so the docs can't drift from the API.

  • Get your keys: sign up, complete KYC, and take live and test keys from the dashboard
  • Register a webhook: one HTTPS endpoint, every event signed with HMAC-SHA256
  • Initiate a collection: one POST returns a checkout URL for the app your customer already uses
  • Money confirmed: the webhook fires on completion and the ledger updates

Common questions

Payment gateway FAQ

Which payment methods does HPG support in The Gambia?

Wave and APS mobile money, plus card payments, all through the same API and reconciled on one ledger in dalasi (GMD).

How long does it take to integrate?

The integration is three requests. The quickstart gets a first test payment through in about 10 minutes, and most teams go from sign-up to a live payment in an afternoon.

How much does HPG cost?

One commission per successful transaction, set by rail and monthly volume. There is no setup fee, no monthly fee, and nothing is charged on failed payments. Contact us to agree your rate.

Can HexAI integrate HPG into our existing system for us?

Yes. Our software team can integrate HPG into your website, app or business system, or build the system around it.
