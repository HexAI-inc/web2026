HexAI Payment Gateway (HPG): Multi-Tenant Wave Integration

A production-ready multi-tenant payment gateway microservice enabling any Gambian business to accept and disburse Wave Mobile Money payments through a single, managed API. HPG handles the full lifecycle — checkout initiation, webhook verification, background payout processing via Redis-backed queues, and a real-time partner dashboard. Built with Next.js, Express, and Prisma, it is the financial rails layer powering HexAI's ecosystem partners.

Multi-tenant architecture — each partner operates in full isolation with unique API keys

Admin portal: revenue charts, transaction monitoring, payout management, webhook audit trail

EdTech & Rural Access

Base10: Offline-First Education Platform for Rural Africa

An offline-first exam prep platform built for rural African students with $40 smartphones and unreliable 3G connectivity. Base10 delivers 4,000+ WAEC past questions across 15 subjects, a smart prioritisation algorithm that surfaces weak topics, and a full offline sync engine — students download question sets on WiFi, study offline, and sync results when they reconnect. Recognised at IndabaX Sierra Leone, Base10 is the blueprint for AI-accessible education on the continent.

Offline sync engine: pull weak-topic questions on WiFi, push attempts on reconnect
Smart algorithm prioritises topics below 50% accuracy to close learning gaps
Phone-first auth — SMS users register without email, 7-day tokens for offline use
Streak and gamification system to drive daily engagement in low-resource contexts
FastAPI + PostgreSQL backend with Docker deployment; React Native mobile app in Phase 2

Kernel: The Business Operating System for African SMEs

Kernel was born from a real failure: in Q1 2026 a client asked HexAI for every invoice ever issued to them, and we couldn't produce it — the records lived on a single laptop that became corrupted. Kernel makes that impossible. It's a cloud-native business operating system covering invoices, bills, contacts, payments, bank reconciliation, and audit-grade reporting — designed so a pharmacy in Serrekunda with a part-time bookkeeper never loses its financial history. HexAI is customer zero; the product is built to be sold across West African SMEs.

End-to-end financial system: invoices, bills, contacts, payments, and bank transactions
Immutable audit logs and reporting — no data trapped on a single device or in one person's head

Built for low-bandwidth West Africa with SSR and resilient sync

Fintech & SME Analytics

FinsaFi: OCR-Powered Financial Analytics for Microenterprises

FinsaFi turns a shoebox of paper receipts into clean, analyzable financial data. Small business owners snap or upload a receipt and PaddleOCR extracts the transaction automatically — with duplicate detection and validation to keep the books trustworthy. Google Gemini layers on plain-language financial insights and recommendations, while P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow reports export to PDF and Excel. Built mobile-first with a camera-first flow and offline-safe upload queueing for owners working on the go.

Receipt OCR via PaddleOCR (PP-OCRv4) — JPG, PNG, and PDF extraction
Financial trust layer: hash-based duplicate detection and anomaly validation
AI insights and recommendations powered by Google Gemini
P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow reports with PDF/Excel export
Offline-safe upload queueing and organization-level RBAC data isolation
